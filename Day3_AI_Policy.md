# Day 3 — AI Use Policy for Placement Cell

## 1. Scenario Classification

| # | Scenario | Tier | Reasoning |
|---|----------|------|-----------|
| 1 | AI-generated cover letter | Limited | Allowed with disclosure. Student's intent is theirs; AI only polished the language. |
| 2 | AI used to solve coding OA rounds | Unacceptable | Direct cheating in a recruiter-administered assessment. Banned outright. |
| 3 | AI-summarised company research for GD/PI | Minimal | Free use. Personal prep tool; no recruiter is misled. |
| 4 | AI-translated interview answers | Limited | Allowed with awareness. Language barrier aid, not skill fabrication. |
| 5 | AI-generated LinkedIn bio and headline | Limited | Allowed with disclosure. Recruiter can verify claims in interview. |
| 6 | AI used to fake internship experience | Unacceptable | Identity and record falsification. Banned outright. |
| 7 | AI chatbot for HR behavioural practice | Minimal | Free use. Personal rehearsal tool; no external party is affected. |
| 8 | AI-generated project documentation | High-risk | Allowed only if student can orally defend every section of the project. |

## 2. Permitted Uses

1. AI may be used to rewrite and polish cover letters for clarity and grammar.
2. AI may be used to simulate mock HR and behavioural interview practice.
3. AI may be used to summarise company background research before GD/PI rounds.
4. AI may be used to translate or improve English phrasing for non-native speakers.
5. AI may be used to draft LinkedIn bio and headline when all claims are factually accurate.
6. AI may be used to generate project documentation for projects the student actually built.
7. AI may be used to create placement preparation schedules and study plans.

## 3. Prohibited Uses

1. AI may NOT be used to solve online assessments or coding rounds during recruitment.
   **Detection method:** Recruiters use plagiarism tools and conduct follow-up technical interviews on the same problems.

2. AI may NOT be used to fabricate internship experience or projects not actually completed.
   **Detection method:** Mentor oral defence — student must explain every project and internship with specific technical decisions.

3. AI may NOT be used to clone or alter the student's voice for video interviews.
   **Detection method:** Cross-verify with recruiter; most platforms now require a live segment alongside recorded answers.

4. AI may NOT be used to generate or falsify CGPA, marks, or academic records.
   **Detection method:** All academic claims cross-referenced with the college academic portal before placement support is given.

## 4. Enforcement

The placement cell will conduct random oral defences on portfolio projects, sampling 20% of students before each placement drive. Any confirmed violation of Prohibited Uses results in a written warning for the first offence and withdrawal of placement support for the current cycle on the second offence. The placement cell acknowledges that not all AI use is detectable and this policy relies in part on student integrity and their awareness of the long-term career consequences of misrepresentation.
