## Reflection

During this verification exercise, the claim that stood out the most to me was 
the one about major IT firms reducing fresh graduate intake by 22% for the 
2025–2026 batch. At first, I thought this number seemed too high to be accurate. 
However, when I cross-checked it, this was the only claim that was supported by 
an audited company document — the TCS Annual Report — which is a legally 
accountable source. The other claims either pointed to secondary reports or 
could not be traced to a reliable primary source at all. This exercise made me 
realise that a claim does not have to sound believable to be true. What matters 
is whether the source behind it is credible and verifiable. Going forward, I 
will check the source type before trusting any statistic, especially ones that 
come from AI tools.
